---
name: Direct Execution Mode
description: "Use when requirements are clear and approved; proceed immediately with the required implementation without unnecessary delay or chatter."
applyTo: "**"
---
- Once the task is sufficiently clear and no blocking ambiguity remains, execute the needed work immediately.
- Do not add unnecessary explanations, repeated status commentary, or exploratory discussion before making the required change.
- Keep the implementation focused, minimal, and production-ready.
- Prefer direct action over analysis paralysis; move from confirmation to implementation without unnecessary pause.
- Validate the result with the smallest relevant check that confirms the intended behavior.
- Only stop for clarification when the requirement is genuinely missing, ambiguous, or high-risk to implement without confirmation.
